# Product continuation review protocol

The user has accepted scoring satisfaction. This review will not reopen that decision or require a new score-payoff validation gate. The scoring game remains kills × mult, with automatic visual tricks. The requested scoreboard has three unboxed components at the bottom.

This rubric is broader and more focused on the player-facing interface than the earlier seven-category mechanics rubric. Its numerical results must not be presented as a direct continuation of the old R4/C4 scores.

| Category | Weight | Evidence required |
|---|---:|---|
| Visual hierarchy and coherent identity | 20% | Actual rendered shop/game screens; clear semantic colour and legible foreground |
| Shop comparison and interaction | 20% | Owned rack visible, recognisable art, short effects, correct price/merge/replacement, actual DOM transactions |
| Onboarding and flow | 15% | Nonblocking tips tied to actual state; correct controls/timer objective and dismiss behaviour |
| Scoring-build communication | 15% | Honest owned-trick/combination relevance, preview scope, explicit conditional effects |
| Fairness and launch-route evidence | 10% | Full-run failure attribution and disclosed natural/constructed comparisons; no bot-win difficulty target |
| Mechanical integrity | 10% | Independent chips×mult, shop accounting, boss lifecycle, overtime and movement checks |
| Mobile usability | 5% | Browser landscape layout, touch checks, visible critical information; emulation limitation stated |
| Feasibility and performance evidence | 5% | Bounded local checks and honest physical-device limits |

Unmeasured: fresh-human learning time, personal enjoyment, retention, commercial appeal in a live market, physical Android frame pacing and thermal performance. Passing DOM or CPU tests cannot establish these.

QA source: `qa/product-review.cjs`. Built hashes are recorded on every report. Failed initial results remain preserved. Test-fixture corrections must be distinguished from source fixes.
