ICARUS flow study

What actually happens in a flight in the current demo: where the dolphin is, where enemies are, how long each part lasts, and when things die. Measured from 291 bot flights, not from the design doc.

How this was measured, and its limits
  • The S4 demo build, run headless by the test bot at 120 steps per second, with positions logged 10 times per second. Screenshots come from the same bot driving the real renderer.
  • Four styles, one seed each: low hop (~30°), mid (~55°), high (~80°), and mid turning round at launch. The bot starts with the harpoon and buys weapons plus Armour, Max HP, Regen, Damage and Shot.
  • In the air the bot keeps about 8 m from the nearest enemy, dodges shots it notices (3 in 4), and collects orbs when safe. It is not you. Read every number as "what a cautious, average bot does".
  • All four bot runs ended on the quota at waves 7–11, so there's nothing here past wave 11 and nothing on bosses. The wave 9 screenshots were skipped because the browser run lost before wave 9.

1. The loop: a short dive, then a flight

Median seconds per flight. The dive is short at every height. Air time grows with the wave because launch speed rises 5 m/s per wave.

UnderwaterIn the air
{{CYCLE}}

Launch decides almost everything. Launch speed is fixed per wave. The angle at release sets the threat tier (about 30° → tier 2, 55° → 3, 80° or more → 5), the peak height (19 m for a wave 1 low hop, up to 128 m for a wave 7+ high launch) and the air time. After release you steer only inside the arena that travels along the arc.

2. Where things are in the arena

The arena is 76 × 43.2 m and rides along the ballistic arc. The camera follows the arena, not the dolphin. The dashed box is the visible 57 m width; the green ring is a 30 m weapon range around the arena centre. Darker cells were occupied more often.

Enemies come in from the side edges of the arena, mostly outside the screen; the median enemy sits 31 m from the centre horizontally. The bot drifts a median of 18 m off centre and sometimes reaches the edge, which puts the dolphin at the edge of the screen. That's visible in the wave 5 climb frame below.

7. What I now understand the flow to be

  1. It's a fast loop. A dive of 1–3 seconds, then a 2–8 second flight. At wave 1 a low hop is about a 3.5 second cycle.
  2. The decision is made at release. Angle sets tier, height and air time all at once. Everything after that is positioning inside a box riding the arc.
  3. The fight is at the edge of the screen. Enemies spawn at the arena's sides and die at roughly weapon range, so the middle of the screen is quiet and the action is at its borders.
  4. The surface is the busiest place. Pursuers wait at the water, so kills start at breach, and 3–10 gather at the landing point (up to 8 follow underwater at waves 7+).
  5. No phase is special yet. Kills follow time evenly. A trick placed at apex today would hit whatever happens to be near.
  6. Hits are rare for a cautious player. The bot took 59 hits in 291 flights, and 83% of its flights had no hit at all. Anything that relies on "a hit cancels the trick" depends on how often real players get hit, which this bot can't tell us.
